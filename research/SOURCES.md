## Generated artwork

The homepage roadway sculpture was created with the built-in ImageGen tool and compressed to WebP as `assets/images/roadway-study.webp`. It illustrates the subject of the portfolio.

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent homepage hero artwork for a senior product designer's automotive logistics portfolio. Create a photorealistic editorial studio render of a single physical sculpture: two sweeping, precision-machined metal roadway ramps crossing at different elevations over a dark olive-gray architectural relief slab. One tiny unbranded ivory model vehicle on the upper roadway for scale, no other objects. Brushed champagne aluminum top surfaces, blackened edges, small parallel engraved lane markings and sparse topographic grooves in the base. A tightly composed wide isometric three-quarter view, viewed slightly from above, the composition reads clearly as a miniature transport interchange and an industrial design object. Sculpture spans 88 percent of a 4:3 landscape frame, fully visible with generous transparent space around its silhouette. Soft directional studio lighting, tactile satin metal and real machined detail, strong edge highlights, deep controlled shadows within the object. Sophisticated architecture model photographed for a design monograph. True alpha transparency around the whole object, no floor, no drop shadow outside the object, no background, no typography, no watermark, no gradients in the transparent area, no neon, no glass, no floating ornaments.

The earlier connected-routes image is abstract portfolio artwork. Generation used the built-in image tool. The archived site asset is `assets/images/connected-routes.webp`. `social-card.webp` uses the same artwork for link previews.

Initial prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: sculptural hero artwork for Kathryn Patterson's product-design portfolio, inspired by the refined dark sculptural mood of designwhich.works. Create a photorealistic studio render of three elegant interlocking continuous metal ribbon loops that suggest a complex route becoming connected. One sculptural object, asymmetric but balanced, open negative space through the loops, brushed champagne-silver metal and one softly matte muted sage-green ceramic band. Sophisticated physical materials, tiny imperfections, no glass. Floating against a perfectly uniform charcoal-black #11130f background. Soft top-left light, subtle edge highlights, very restrained floor shadow. Center object occupies 72 percent of image with generous margins, full object visible, landscape 4:3 composition. Museum design object, tactile, calm, sophisticated. This is abstract artwork, NOT a product interface or diagram. No text, letters, UI, labels, branding, watermark, neon, glow, gradients, decorative particles, or extra props.

The final hero uses a targeted transparent-background derivative so that the same sculpture works in both themes. The social preview retains the charcoal-background original.

Edit prompt:

> Use case: background-extraction. This image is the edit target. Remove the charcoal studio background and floor shadow completely to produce a genuinely transparent-background cutout of the exact interlocking metal and sage ceramic sculpture. Preserve the sculpture's exact geometry, brushed metal textures, colors, highlight lighting, proportions, orientation, and framing. Preserve holes between loops as alpha transparency. No redesign, no added objects, no text, no new shadow, no matte or halo around edges. Output one transparent cutout suitable for both light and dark website surfaces.
