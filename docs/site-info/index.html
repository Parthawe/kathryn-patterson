A few details about this portfolio.

Content and images

Professional information is drawn from Kathryn's public LinkedIn profile. Central Dispatch imagery comes from its public product guide. Cync material comes from the University of Georgia capstone archive. Project pages link to those sources.

The connected-routes hero sculpture is AI-generated abstract artwork. It does not represent a product interface or research artifact.

Privacy

This site has no contact form, advertising pixels, or analytics scripts. Your theme choice is stored in your browser. Visiting external links is subject to those services' policies. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages.

Accessibility

The site includes keyboard navigation, visible focus states, image descriptions, reduced-motion support, and light and dark themes. Images can be enlarged with a keyboard-accessible viewer.

Contact Kathryn through LinkedIn if you need another way to access the material.
